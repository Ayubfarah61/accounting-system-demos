# Accounting System Demo Copies

Two offline, read-only portfolio showcase copies inspired by accounting systems Ayub built for different workflows:

## Live demos

- [Demo hub](https://ayubfarah61.github.io/accounting-system-demos/)
- [Trading & manufacturing](https://ayubfarah61.github.io/accounting-system-demos/trading/)
- [Accounting & operations](https://ayubfarah61.github.io/accounting-system-demos/operations/)

- **Trading & Manufacturing** — bookkeeping, stock, production, settlements, and reporting.
- **Accounting & Operations** — general accounting, gold operations, payroll, equipment, rentals, and reporting.

## Privacy and demo scope

These are standalone frontend demonstrations of selected screens and workflows, not copies of production source code or complete accounting applications. The fictional business name is **ABC Co Ltd**. Every amount, name, and transaction is made up and shown in **USD**. The demos do not connect to production systems, APIs, databases, accounts, or Cloudflare. They have no login, write actions, analytics, or external runtime dependencies. They do not contain business exports, backups, credentials, or production configuration.

Open `index.html` locally, or publish this folder as a static site. The two demos are in `trading/` and `operations/`.

## Important

The demo pages illustrate selected workflows and reporting screens; they are not accounting software and must not be used to record or rely on real transactions. The live applications remain separate and are not included.
